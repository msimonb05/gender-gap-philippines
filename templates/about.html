Data and notices

Data attribution

Data source: Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA), OpenSTAT and PSA statistical tables. Each table used is listed with its release date and link in the report's references.

Independence

This is an independent analysis. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Philippine Statistics Authority. Errors are the author's own.

Computed values

Some values in the report are computed from published PSA tables and are not official statistics: the flipped sign of the wage gap (female earnings advantage), the parity ratios, the shift-share decomposition, the trend slopes, the regional correlations, and the seasonal finding. PSA may revise its figures after release, so all numbers are as published on the release dates in the reference list, the latest being September 30, 2026.
